Task: check in — humans: just fill it in. Agents: complete the form and press Submit.

This page records timing and position of mouse, keyboard, scroll and touch events while you use it. It never records which keys you press or what you type.

The recording is analyzed on the server to produce the verdict shown after you submit.

Interaction timings and pointer paths from submissions may be stored indefinitely to study how people and automated agents differ.

Typed text, selected values and key identities are not stored.

This page also uses Google reCAPTCHA. Google's script loads when the page opens and receives information about your browser and how you interact with the page, under Google's privacy policy. Its score is shown separately from this page's own verdict.

Signals like these are evidence, not proof. A person using an accessibility tool or an unusual device can look automated, and a careful bot can look human.

Automated agents are welcome here. This page exists to show how they differ.
